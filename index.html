About

My research combines satellite Earth observation, field measurements, and machine learning to investigate the inland waters (lentic and lotic systems) and their storage dynamics from local to global scales. I build end-to-end, data-driven processing pipelines that combine multi-source satellite imagery and apply machine-learning models for understanding geospatial hydrology and developing decision-support applications.

What I work on

Mapping inland waters

The ADEMA framework: an automated, data-efficient morphometric approach to classify global lentic and lotic inland waters at 0.09 hectare resolution.

Storage and flood implications

The Tankshed Overflow Index and basin-scale analysis linking small waterbody storage to flood risk, applied to the Adyar-Chennai basin.

Observations: Landsat · Sentinel-1/2 · ICESat-2 · satellite altimetry · Google Earth Engine

ADEMA: mapping inland waters from morphology

Estimating storage in small and medium inland waters
